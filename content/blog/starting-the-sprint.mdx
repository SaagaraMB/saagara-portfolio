I'm starting this site at the same time as the hands-on part of my
transition into physical AI — deliberately, rather than waiting until
there's a polished project to show.

The plan for the next few weeks is Pick-and-Place using SO-101: collect my own teleoperated
pick-and-place demonstrations on a SO-101 arm, fine-tune and compare two
different policy architectures (ACT and SmolVLA) on the same data, and
evaluate both honestly against a published reference. After that, an RL
Token phase extends whichever policy comes out stronger.

I'll be updating the [Pick-and-Place using SO-101 project page](/projects/pick-and-place-so-101) as real
results come in, rather than writing this post again once it's done. If
something doesn't work the way I expect, that will show up here too — the
point of documenting this in public is the honest account, not just the
finished demo.
