## Core idea

Fresh lunar simple craters — young, bowl-shaped craters that haven't yet been
worn down by subsequent impacts — are usually treated as roughly circular.
In practice their rim crests are often measurably asymmetric. This paper
builds a systematic, quantitative measurement of that rim crest asymmetry
across a set of fresh simple craters, using high-resolution digital
elevation models derived from Chandrayaan-2's Terrain Mapping Camera-2
(TMC-2).
