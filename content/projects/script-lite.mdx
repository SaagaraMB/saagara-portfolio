## Goal

Test whether a robot arm can learn individual handwritten letters from
teleoperated demonstration and then *compose* them into a word it was
never shown as a complete sequence — a small, concrete probe into
compositional generalization for manipulation policies.

## Approach

1. Collect teleoperated demonstrations of individual handwritten letters
   on a whiteboard.
2. Fine-tune a vision-language-action policy on the per-letter
   demonstrations.
3. Test the policy on a held-out word built entirely from letters it has
   seen individually, but never in that order — the generalization test
   that matters.

## Status

SCRIPT-Lite starts after BAT-Lite's core work (fine-tuning, evaluation, and
the RL Token phase) is finished. This page will be filled in with the real
setup, results, and a demo once the project is underway.
